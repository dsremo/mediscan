# Gemini Deep Research Prompt — Gemma 4 Good Hackathon

Copy this into Gemini Deep Research:

---

I'm competing in the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon on Kaggle ($200K prize, deadline May 18, 2026). I need to build the most impactful project using Google's Gemma 4 model. Help me research:

### 1. Gemma 4 Technical Capabilities
- What are Gemma 4's exact multimodal capabilities? (image understanding, OCR, etc.)
- How does native function calling work in Gemma 4?
- What are the model sizes (2B, 7B, 27B) and their tradeoffs?
- Can Gemma 4 run offline on Android phones or Raspberry Pi?
- What quantization options exist (GGUF, GPTQ, AWQ)?
- How does Gemma 4 compare to Gemma 3 and Llama 3?

### 2. Winning Hackathon Projects (Past Gemma Competitions)
- What won the Gemma 3n Impact Challenge? (the previous Gemma hackathon on Kaggle)
- What won the Google Tunix Hack?
- What common traits do winning hackathon projects share?
- What made winning demos stand out?

### 3. Healthcare + AI for Developing Countries
- What are the biggest healthcare gaps where AI could help in rural/developing areas?
- Are there existing open-source medical image datasets for skin conditions, eye diseases, wound assessment?
- What regulations exist for medical AI in developing countries?
- What existing apps do this? (e.g., Ada Health, Babylon, etc.) What are their limitations?

### 4. Edge Deployment of Gemma 4
- How to run Gemma 4 2B/7B on Android (llama.cpp, MediaPipe, MLKit)?
- What's the inference speed on typical smartphones?
- How to do offline multimodal inference (image + text) on mobile?
- Examples of Gemma deployed on edge devices

### 5. Competition Landscape
- What are the other 36 teams likely building?
- What focus area has the least competition?
- What would a $50K winning project look like vs a $5K one?

Output should include specific GitHub repos, arxiv papers, app examples, and technical benchmarks.
