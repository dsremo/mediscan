# MediScan — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

**Total: 3 minutes max. Video is 30% of the score — make it compelling.**

---

## [0:00 - 0:20] THE HOOK (20 seconds)

**Visual:** Slow pan over a rural village in Africa/India. A community health worker walks between simple homes carrying a phone.

**Voiceover:**
> "3.5 billion people have no access to basic healthcare. In Sub-Saharan Africa, there's less than one skin doctor for every million people. But almost everyone has a phone."

**Visual:** Close-up of a smartphone screen showing MediScan logo.

> "What if that phone could help save lives?"

---

## [0:20 - 0:50] THE PROBLEM (30 seconds)

**Visual:** Quick montage — a child with visible skin condition, a long line at a rural clinic, a map showing distance to nearest hospital.

**Voiceover:**
> "Community health workers are the frontline of care in underserved regions. They see hundreds of patients, but most lack training to assess skin conditions — the most common health issue in developing countries. A wrong assessment can mean a child with scabies infects their entire school. A missed burn infection can become life-threatening."

**Visual:** Health worker looking uncertain at a patient's arm, no tools to help.

> "Existing health apps need internet. In rural areas, there is none."

---

## [0:50 - 2:00] THE SOLUTION — LIVE DEMO (70 seconds)

**Visual:** Screen recording of MediScan in action.

**Voiceover:**
> "Meet MediScan. Powered by Google Gemma 4, it runs entirely on your phone — no internet needed."

**[Demo 1: Scabies Case]** (25 seconds)
- Show typing: "Small itchy bumps between fingers, worse at night. Family members have it too."
- Click Analyze
- Show the structured response: Scabies, HIGH urgency, treat all family members, seek prescription treatment
> "In seconds, the health worker knows this is likely scabies — highly contagious, needs prescription treatment, and the entire household needs to be treated at once."

**[Demo 2: Burn Case with Image]** (25 seconds)
- Upload a stock image of a minor burn
- Type: "Burned hand on cooking fire yesterday"
- Show response: Second degree burn, HIGH, cool water 10-20 min, don't pop blisters, seek care if larger than palm
> "Gemma 4 analyzes both the image and the description together. It gives specific, culturally relevant first aid — not textbook advice, but what actually works in a village setting."

**[Demo 3: Function Calling]** (20 seconds)
- Show the structured JSON output
- Show medication reminder being set
> "Behind the scenes, Gemma 4's native function calling produces structured triage data — urgency level, confidence score, follow-up questions. This integrates directly with health information systems."

---

## [2:00 - 2:30] THE TECHNOLOGY (30 seconds)

**Visual:** Architecture diagram animation.

**Voiceover:**
> "MediScan uses Gemma 4 E4B — a 4.5 billion parameter model that fits on a phone. We fine-tuned it with Unsloth on skin disease data from 22 conditions, optimized for the diseases that matter most in tropical and underserved regions."

**Visual:** Show model running on phone (or Raspberry Pi footage).

> "Quantized to 4-bit, it runs at 12 to 20 tokens per second on a standard Android phone. All data stays on-device. Zero cloud dependency. Complete privacy."

---

## [2:30 - 2:55] THE IMPACT (25 seconds)

**Visual:** Health worker smiling, using MediScan with a patient. Patient receives treatment.

**Voiceover:**
> "MediScan doesn't replace doctors. It empowers the community health workers who are already there — giving them the tools to triage accurately, prioritize urgent cases, and provide life-saving first aid before a patient can reach a clinic."

> "One phone. One model. Healthcare for millions who have never had it."

---

## [2:55 - 3:00] CLOSE (5 seconds)

**Visual:** MediScan logo + tagline.

> "MediScan. Healthcare in your pocket — no internet required."

**Text on screen:**
- Built with Google Gemma 4
- github.com/dsremo/mediscan
- Gemma 4 Good Hackathon 2026

---

## Production Notes

- **Record screen demos** using OBS or phone screen recorder
- **Stock footage** for rural/medical scenes: Pexels, Pixabay (free, no attribution needed)
- **Music:** Uplifting but subtle background — YouTube Audio Library has free options
- **Edit in:** CapCut (free), DaVinci Resolve (free), or Canva Video
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **Upload to YouTube** as unlisted or public
